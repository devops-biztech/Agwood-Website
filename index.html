Supplying Redwood. Supporting Builders. Since 1954.

At Agwood Mill &amp; Lumber, we specialize in one thing-redwood. For more than 70 years, our Ukiah mill has produced premium redwood lumber for decking, fencing, and custom applications. We partner with trusted retailers and distributors across Northern California to bring redwood's beauty, strength, and durability to builders, contractors, and homeowners.

Redwood is a California classic-sustainable, workable, and timeless. At Agwood, we've built our reputation on milling it with precision and consistency, ensuring our partners get reliable supply and unmatched quality.
